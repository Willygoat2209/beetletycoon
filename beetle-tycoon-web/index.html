❓ How to play

🐞 BuyBeetles crawl down the Bug Trail. Walk up to one and buy it. It goes into your base.
💰 EarnEvery beetle makes money each second. Step on its green pad to collect.
🦹 StealSneak into a rival base, grab a beetle and run home. Don't get caught!
🔒 DefendRivals raid your base. Bump into a thief to get your beetle back, or step on LOCK.
🌧️ WeatherStorms and golden hours make rare mutations like Gold, Diamond and Cosmic more common.
✨ RebirthGet rich, rebirth, and earn more money forever.

Move: WASD / arrows / joystick · Action: E / tap the button · Jump: Space · Zoom: mouse wheel
